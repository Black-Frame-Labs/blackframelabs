Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-08

Overview

This application ("Black Recorder") respects your privacy. The app is designed to function fully offline and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

The application does not collect any personal or sensitive user data.

  • No personal identifiers are collected.
  • No analytics are used.
  • No advertising SDKs are included.
  • No tracking technologies are present.

Audio Recordings

  • Audio recordings are stored locally on your device only.
  • Recordings are never uploaded or transmitted outside the device.
  • You can delete recordings at any time from within the app or by uninstalling the app.

The application does not access recordings without explicit user interaction.

Permissions

The application requests the following permission:

  • Microphone access - used solely to record audio when you explicitly start a recording.

No other permissions are requested.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party services that collect user data, including analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud services.

Data Security

Because the application does not collect or transmit data, no personal data is stored or processed by the developer. All recordings remain under the control of the user on their own device.

Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the application functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
